About

From first principles to markets.

Physics trains you to break a hard problem down to first principles before you trust any answer you find. That habit is what pulled Krispin toward the problems he now finds most interesting — markets, companies, and how businesses actually work at scale.

An international student from Western Rwanda, he came to Amherst College to study physics and found that the instinct to solve real-world problems translates directly into business and finance.

Outside the classroom, he builds software products — treating each one as a working model of a real system, and a way to learn how businesses actually operate.

Every morning starts with the Financial Times and the Wall Street Journal — not just for the headlines, but to see how social and geopolitical events move the markets.
